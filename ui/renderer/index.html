Emulator

USB keyboard

Plug a USB or Bluetooth keyboard into the PC. SDL sees it as a keyboard; no driver or pairing is needed inside Prospero.

  • Click the game window once so it has focus.
  • Every binding is passed to the emulator as --keymap Control=Key.
  • F8 cycles the keyboard player at runtime; the default shares player 1 with a gamepad.
  • F6 toggles touch-pad mode, F7 toggles mouse-to-right-stick.

Keybindings

Click a binding, then press a key or mouse button. Esc cancels. F1, F2, F7, F8 and F11 are reserved.
